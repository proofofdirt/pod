HS Codes: 0104.10 (sheep), 0104.20 (goats) | Unit: USD/kg live weight
Sources: selinawamucii.com, UN COMTRADE, OEC, trade contacts

---

## Context

Live small ruminants are PoD's primary export product from the Ethiopia corridor.
Ethiopian Rift Valley and southern lowlands are significant sources for KSA/UAE markets.
Pricing is highly seasonal (Hajj/Eid spikes), origin-sensitive (disease ban risk),
and grade-dependent (live weight, body condition score, age/sex).

## Ethiopian Origin Premium/Discount Factors

| Factor | Impact |
|---|---|
| Halal certified slaughter at origin | +$0.10-0.20/kg |
| Live animal (vs frozen carcass) | Buyer preference varies by market |
| Body condition score < 3.0 | -$0.30-0.60/kg or rejection |
| Active Rift Valley Fever (RVF) in Ethiopia | Full ban (see trade-restrictions/) |
| FMD status | Affects certain markets -- monitor OIE |
| Quarantine at Jeddah (21 days) | Adds $0.15-0.25/kg logistics cost |
