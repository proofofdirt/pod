Multisig & Elections

Five seats. Twelve-month terms.

STRUCTURE

3-of-5 multisig

Signers represent the full chain: developers who build the trust tech, producers / field operations who grow the product, and buyers who take it to market — plus the coordinator and an external advisor. No single party controls the treasury or the rules.

DECISIONS

What requires approval

Treasury disbursements, bounty payouts, oracle parameter changes, financing rules, signer changes, and any future recordkeeping instrument — all clear the multisig; major policy changes carry a 7-day community notice.

ELECTIONS

Annual signer election

Every 12 months each seat goes to a community election: contributors vote to keep or replace the signer. Results are executed on-chain as a Squads member-change proposal — the election outcome and the key rotation are both permanent public records.

ELECTION MECHANICS ARE CONFIGURED AT MULTISIG SETUP (SQUADS, SOLANA) · SEE GOVERNANCE CHARTER ON GITHUB
