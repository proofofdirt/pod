AI Researchers

Beat our baselines.

We publish labeled satellite datasets and run open competitions — Zindi, Kaggle, AI for Good — to push SAR and NDVI detection past our in-house models. Prize money is paid only for verified improvement, and every winning model ships open source. Small-plot accuracy (1–10 ha) is the frontier we care most about.
