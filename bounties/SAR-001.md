## Objective

Build an open-source ML model using Copernicus Sentinel-1 C-band SAR to detect mechanical soil disturbance (plowing / tillage) events with **>90% accuracy** on Ethiopian plots of **1 hectare and above**.

Report performance by plot-size band (1–10 ha, 10–100 ha, 100 ha+). Sentinel-1's 10 m resolution makes small plots the hard part — document limitations honestly.

---

## Why This Matters

Before any oracle-gated tranche disbursement for tractor work is approved, the Oracle must confirm that plowing actually happened on the claimed date and location.

> **Architectural constraint (V1):** Ethiopian law prohibits stablecoin/cryptocurrency transactions. The Oracle does **not** move money. It writes verified data on-chain; a confirmed milestone emits a tranche approval event, and PODRWA SPV (KSA) executes the disbursement as a **fiat bank wire** to Turmi (or another Ethiopian operator). Fully on-chain settlement is a V2 goal, contingent on Ethiopian regulation.

Optical satellites are blind 70%+ of the time in Ethiopia's tropical growing seasons due to cloud cover. Sentinel-1 SAR penetrates cloud — it's the only practical verification source for plowing in this geography.

A false positive (approving a disbursement for plowing that didn't happen) or a false negative (blocking a legitimate disbursement) both have material financial consequences, at any plot size. The 90% accuracy threshold is a floor, not a target.

## Technical Approach

The core signal is **VV/VH backscatter ratio change before and after the claimed plowing date**. Plowing creates characteristic soil surface roughness that appears as a distinct SAR backscatter increase.

**Recommended approach:**
1. Acquire pre-event (T-14 to T-3 days) and post-event (T+1 to T+10 days) Sentinel-1 GRD scenes over the polygon
2. Apply terrain correction (RD or RTC) using SRTM DEM
3. Compute per-pixel log-ratio: `ΔdB = 10 * log10(σ_post / σ_pre)`
4. Extract polygon statistics (mean, variance, percentiles) from ΔdB raster
5. On larger plots, spatial variance within the polygon is highly informative — plowing creates characteristic heterogeneity patterns; for 1–10 ha plots lean on pixel-level time-series features
6. Train a classifier (RF or lightweight CNN) on the ground truth set
7. Output confidence score calibrated against the validation set

**Sentinel-1 repeat pass:** 12-day cycle over Ethiopia — plan acquisition windows accordingly.
